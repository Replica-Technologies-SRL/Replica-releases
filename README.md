# Replica Desktop

Official downloads for **Replica**, the desktop trade copier for futures traders.

Replica watches the fills on your leader account and places the same trades on as many
follower accounts as you like, at the size you choose. It works with **Tradovate**,
**ProjectX (TopstepX)** and **Rithmic**, and adds per-account risk rules, one-click
Flatten and a trading Journal. Everything runs on your own computer.

## Updates

You only install Replica once. When a new version comes out, the app offers it to you the
next time you open it. Every update is checked against Replica's signing key before it is
installed.
