# Replica Desktop

Official downloads for **Replica**, the desktop trade copier for futures traders.

Replica watches the fills on your leader account and places the same trades on as many
follower accounts as you like, at the size you choose. It works with **Tradovate**,
**ProjectX (TopstepX)** and **Rithmic**, and adds per-account risk rules (Guardrails),
trading-hours lockouts, one-click Flatten and a trading Journal. Everything runs on your
own computer.

**Latest version: 0.1.55** (9 November 2026), for Windows and macOS.

**Chip: Apple M…**, take Apple silicon. If it says **Processor: Intel**, take Intel.

You can always find the newest version on the

or at [replicacopier.com](https://replicacopier.com).

## Install

- **Windows:** run `Replica-Installer.exe` and follow the steps.
- **macOS:** open the `.dmg` and drag **Replica** into **Applications**, then open it from
  Applications.

The Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## Updates

You only install Replica once. When a new version comes out, the app offers it to you the
next time you open it, on Windows and on Mac. Every update is checked against Replica's
signing key before it is installed.

Keep Replica open while you trade: copying and risk rules only work while the app is running.
